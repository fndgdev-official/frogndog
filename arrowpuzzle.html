Gameplay

A small move.
A little more space.

  1. Find an open path.

    Look in the direction an arrow points. It needs a clear route to leave the board.

  2. Tap to let it go.

    Each arrow you remove makes room for the next. Read the board and choose your order.

  3. Take your time.

    Clear the board at your own pace. Hints are free, with no timer or lives to manage.

Leave a puzzle halfway through and return to the arrows you left. Browser progress saves locally after each arrow. There is no account, cloud backup or Android sync; clearing browser data removes the save.

New clears in levels 1–30 still count toward that daily total, even though those levels show no ads. Clear up to four new levels a day and no ads appear.
