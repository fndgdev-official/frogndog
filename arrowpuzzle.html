Gameplay

A small move.
A little more space.

  1. Find an open path.

    Look in the direction an arrow points. It needs a clear route to leave the board.

  2. Tap to let it go.

    Each arrow you remove makes room for the next. Read the board and choose your order.

  3. Take your time.

    Clear the board at your own pace. Hints are free, with no timer or lives to manage.

Leave a puzzle halfway through and return to the arrows you left. Progress saves after each arrow on your device; there is no account or FNDG cloud sync.

Free to play

Space to solve.
A clear ad policy.

Levels 1–30 show no ads. After those levels, an ad may appear between puzzles on your day's 5th new level clear, then your 15th, 25th and every 10 new clears after that.

New clears in levels 1–30 still count toward that daily total, even though those levels show no ads. Clear up to four new levels a day and no ads appear.

No ads interrupt a puzzle. Unavailable ads are skipped so you can continue. Hints stay free.
